# BusinessPulse — Business Requirements

## 1. Business Scenario

BusinessPulse Retail is an e-commerce company that sells products
online across multiple regions.

The company generates large amounts of transactional and customer
data but needs a data-driven system to better understand business
performance and identify areas requiring management attention.

## 2. Business Objective

Build an end-to-end Business Intelligence and Decision Intelligence
platform that helps management understand business performance,
identify important problems, and make data-driven decisions.

## 3. Key Business Questions

### Sales & Revenue
- How is revenue changing over time?
- Which periods perform best and worst?
- What factors are driving revenue changes?

### Profitability
- Which products generate the most profit?
- Which products have high sales but low profitability?
- How do discounts affect profitability?

### Customers
- Who are the most valuable customers?
- Which customers are becoming inactive?
- Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
- What is the customer retention pattern?

### Products
- Which products sell the most?
- Which products generate the most profit?
- Which products have high return rates?

### Regional Performance
- Which regions perform best?
- Which regions are declining?
- What factors explain regional differences?

### Returns
- What is the overall return rate?
- Which products have unusually high return rates?
- What are the main return reasons?

## 4. Expected Outcome

The final solution should provide:

1. Clean and validated business data.
2. SQL-based business analysis.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs).
4. Interactive Power BI dashboards.
5. Identification of important business problems.
6. Root-cause analysis of major findings.
7. AI-assisted business insights.
8. Data-driven recommendations for management.

## 5. Target Users

- Business Managers
- Sales Managers
- Marketing Managers
- Operations Managers
- Senior Management
- Data Analysts
- Business Analysts
